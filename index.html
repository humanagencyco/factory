How it works

🏡 For you

  1. Set your postcode. Once. It's saved in this browser, no account needed.
  2. Check Today. Weather and when the rain starts, the cheapest fuel near you, when to leave for your train, and bin day.
  3. Let it come to you. Turn on reminders, add the page to your home screen, and save home and work so you can switch in one tap.

Tips: tap any card for the full list. ☆ pins a card to the top, ✕ hides one. Coming back shows what's changed since your last visit.

🛠️ For builders

  1. Try any card with a real postcode. It's live public data.
  2. Take it: share a link with the postcode filled in, or add it to your home screen.
  3. Build on it: tap </> Code on a card to install the package and run the example.

🔒 Your data

  • Your postcode, saved places and pinned cards stay in this browser.
  • Reminders: we keep your number (or this device) and your bin address only to send them. Reply STOP BINS on WhatsApp, or turn them off in the Today box.
  • We count visits with a random ID. No cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites.
