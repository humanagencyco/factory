Questions people ask

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with the tools you already pay for, and only suggest buying something when it clearly beats building.

We talk first, agree the scope, then fix the price before any work starts. No day-rate surprises.

What about our data?

It stays in your systems and your accounts. We set permissions and approvals so AI only reads what it should, and people approve what it does.

Our sweet spot is 50 to 500 people. For smaller businesses we have a simpler get found by Google and AI service.
